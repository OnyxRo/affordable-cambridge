We want to live here.

For 100 years, Cambridge made apartment homes illegal in most of the city.

The rules were written to prioritize the wealthy and keep out low-income families and minority residents.

“What Cambridge needs are brick and stone mansions, occupied by millionaires who will share our tax burden.”

Cambridge Rep. Arthur F. Blanchard, 1942

Homes merged into mansions.

In West Cambridge, two and three-family houses were converted to single-family homes.

0homes lost to single-family conversions

City of Cambridge building permits, West Cambridge, 1996 to 2024. How we counted

In 2025, we legalized homes for everyone.

The Multifamily Housing Ordinance made it possible to build apartment homes across all of Cambridge.

The story keeps going: what 2025 changed

Now homes can be built for everyone, not just mansions for the wealthy.

In 2025 we opened every neighborhood to new homes. On a lot where the rules once allowed one large house, four stories now make room for nine families.
